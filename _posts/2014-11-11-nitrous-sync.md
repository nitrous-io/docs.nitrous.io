# Using Nitrous Sync

*Note: By installing the Nitrous.IO CLI you agree to the terms of [Beta Agreement](http://nitrous-io.github.io/docs.nitrous.io/beta-agreement/)*

## Mac OS X

[Download]() and extract Nitrous Sync to your `Applications` folder and open the app. You will be prompted to provide your login credentials at this point.

>Note: If you are receiving a permissions message and are unable to open Nitrous Sync you may need to adjust your security settings. Within **System Preferences > Security & Privacy**, ensure the option **Allow apps downloaded from: Anywhere** is selected.

File Sync: When file sync is enabled, a folder titled `Nitrous/` is created within your home folder. Project folders will be synced within `~/Nitrous/`.
