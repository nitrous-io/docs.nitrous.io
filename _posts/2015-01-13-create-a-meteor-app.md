# Create a Meteor App

<p class="intro">This tutorial covers how to build a Meteor todo app on Nitrous.</p>

## Create a Workspace with a Meteor Template

Login to your Nitrous Dashboard and navigate to [**New > Workspace**](https://pro.nitrous.io/app/#/workspaces/new). Name your workspace, select a region near you, and select a VM size (for this example you can select Nano).

Select the **Meteor** template and click **Create**. Nitrous will take a moment to create your Meteor app.

# Preview Your Starter App

Now that you have a Meteor app created, let's take a look at the code.

Within your app settings in the Dashboard, click the **IDE** link found in the bottom right of your dashboard.

![Open IDE]({{site.baseurl}}/images/articles/getting-started/open-ide.png)

You'll notice in the file tree that there is a starter app already provided in `~/code/starter`. You can preview this app instantly by navigating to **Preview > Port 3000** within the IDE menu bar. Your Meteor app will open in a new window.

![Open IDE]({{site.baseurl}}/images/articles/meteor/welcome-meteor-app.png)
